  • Is the app safe to keep my money and financial data?

    Yes, absolutely. We follow the best cybersecurity and protection standards to make sure your money is kept safe.

  • How are you completely free?

    The most important question! As we are a digital-only bank with no physical branches, our operating expenses are extremely low. We pass to our customers free financial services for all the money that we have been saving. We do not make money from the money of our customers, but rather from the interchange fees we collect from the merchants whenever our customers swipe their prepaid debit card to make offline and online payments. Besides, we earn money from the small commissions we get from the domestic and international transfers.

Cards

  • In the coming weeks, we will automatically start to onboard people from our waitlist.

  • Does the prepaid debit card work for international payments?

    Yes, right in the app, you can enable and disable the international payments.

  • Can I get a virtual card?

    Yes, you can get a virtual card made only for your online lifestyle.

  • What is a numberless card?

    Incumbent banks and financial institutions provide debit cards with the expiration date and CVV but MoneyFlap gives you numberless cards in which the expiration date and CVV are not printed. Thus, we are protecting your sensitive data in case the card is lost or stolen as well as from frauds. But, you will be able to access them in app.

  • Can I make purchases on Netflix, Spotify, Amazon, Jumia, eBay, Aliexpress, Apple..etc?

    Yes! You will be able to purchase on any international websites that accept VISA cards.
