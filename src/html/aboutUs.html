About Us

We are underserved people building a simple, transparent, and modern bank for underserved people.

MoneyFlap is the one-stop-solution for Millennials and Gen Z’s finances, offering everyday
banking services and payments mechanisms they deserve – all within a mobile application.

We mainly focus on innovations that will benefit people living on less than 5 United States Dollar Purchase Power Parity (PPP) a day. We are targeting everyone across Africa, but exceptionally French Africa where nearly 80 percent of the population is still unbanked or underbanked.

The availability of efficient and reliable banking systems is simply not accessible to everyone in Africa particularly French Africa. Meaning, not everyone is financially capable or just quite simply doesn’t have the resources to access banks, this could include things such as high fees, bureaucracy, rows, hassle, and paperwork.

We consider financial inclusion as a human right. We aim to include most of the unbanked people across Africa and beyond. Making financial ease accessible to everyone will help bring communities together and make them healthier. Creating a platform that can financially connect Africa will be an effective way of boosting Africa’s financial infrastructure.
